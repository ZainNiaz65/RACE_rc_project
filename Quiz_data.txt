The Great Barrier Reef is the world's largest coral reef system, composed of over 2,900 individual reefs and 900 islands stretching for over 2,300 kilometers. Climate change poses the greatest threat to the reef, with rising ocean temperatures causing widespread coral bleaching. When water temperatures rise, corals expel the algae living in their tissues, turning completely white.

What causes coral bleaching?

Option A: Too much sunlight

Option B: Rising ocean temperatures

Option C: Overfishing

Option D: Water pollution

Correct Answer: B
